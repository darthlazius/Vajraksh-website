Built around the enclave

Your software.
Your evidence. Your boundary.

Restricted connectivity is a starting condition. The intended architecture keeps assessment and review within your controlled environment.

Offline operation

Local advisory data and assessment tools. No implicit cloud upload of submitted software or evidence.

Disposable execution

Test binaries inside isolated environments, separated from controller credentials and operational networks.

Controlled updates

Bring advisory data and tool updates through your approved offline process, with recorded provenance.

Attributable review

Separate analysis and approval responsibilities. Retain the context behind decisions and exceptions.
